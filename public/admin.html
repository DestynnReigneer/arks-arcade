👪 Profiles

Family profiles for "Who's playing?" on the home page. New ones wait here for you to approve. A PIN is optional; whoever owns the profile can add one, and you can clear a forgotten one.

Wrong PINs lock things for a while, so nobody can guess their way in. Five wrong PINs on one profile locks that profile for 15 minutes, and it shows 🔒 Locked here with an Unlock button. Twenty wrong PINs from one device blocks it for 10 minutes. The button below lifts every device block at once.

🎬 Send a clip

Clips are the GIFs, videos and sounds in the media/clips folder on your server (see docs/CUSTOM-MEDIA.md). Pick one and who gets it, and it pops up the next time their screen is open in the arcade, behind a "tap to open" card. Only profiles can get clips, not guests. If the server restarts before they see it, it's forgotten.

🏠 Home page games

When the box below is ticked, the home page puts the games each person plays most at the top, based on the last 90 days. Someone who hasn't picked a profile, or hasn't played much yet, sees what the whole family plays most. Games nobody has played follow the order you set here. Untick it to always use your order. You can also hide games you don't want showing; hidden games still work if someone has the link.

Working on a game? Turn it off. It stays on the home page, faded, with a "Pardon our dust, under construction" sign, and tapping it says the game is getting some work done. Anyone who opens its page from an old link sees the same sign and a button back to the arcade. Turn it on again when it's ready.

🚂 ARKS Train Station cars

The eight cars on the train, front to back. Each name goes on the car's side and on the light board (its first letter is the board's big letter). Easy cars are long and worth 5, medium ones 15, and hard ones are short and worth 40. Until you save names here, the train uses made-up ones. Names are 1 to 12 letters, numbers or spaces.

📰 Crossword clue packs

Clueless Crosswords only builds its crosswords from words that have a clue. It comes with about a thousand clues written for it, and every pack you add here gives it more words to choose from, so the grids get more varied. The family mini only uses Family clues; the adults' crossword uses both kinds.

🛠️ Make a clue pack

Copy the prompt, paste it into any AI chatbot, then paste its answer below. The words in the prompt come from the arcade's everyday word list, and ones that don't have a clue yet are picked first.

Each clue is checked before it's kept: the word has to be on the family word list (3 to 7 letters), the clue has to be short, it can't contain its own answer, and it can't use a word the kids blocklist removes. Anything that fails is skipped, and you'll see which and why. Today's crosswords are already made, so new clues show up from tomorrow.

🏆 Take a score off a board

If someone puts a rude name on a board, or a score that clearly wasn't earned, you can take just that one score off here. Pick the game and its board, find the row, and tap Remove twice (the first tap asks, the second one does it). The score disappears from the board and from everyone's ranks straight away, but it isn't deleted: it moves to the list under the board, with when it was taken off and by whom, and Put back returns it exactly as it was. Rambler's boards have their own Remove buttons on the Rambler tab.
