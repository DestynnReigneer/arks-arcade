      How the arcade works

      Pick who's playing with your profile at the top left, then tap any game to start. To play together, one person taps Host in a game, and everyone else joins with the 4-letter code or the QR code.

      Each game has its own How to play guide. Tap the ? inside any game to open it, even in the middle of a round.

      Your scores, streaks and high-score spots follow your profile. If you play as a guest, nothing is saved under your name.

      Found something broken, or have an idea? Tap the little bug in the bottom-right corner of any page, or Send feedback at the bottom of this one. You can add a screenshot and draw on it.
