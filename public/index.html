      How the arcade works

      Pick who's playing with your profile at the top left, then tap any game to start. To play multiplayer, one person creates a game lobby. Players can join a hosted multiplayer game from the "List Hosted Games" or "Lobbies" on this page.

      Each game has its own How to play guide. Tap the ? inside any game to open it, even in the middle of a round.

      Scores, streaks and high-score spots go to your profile. A guest's go up under their initials.

      Found something broken, or have an idea? Tap the bug.
